Daqqa Kids

Daqqa Kids makes short, gentle animated videos for children aged 4 to 7: bedtime stories with Shahrazad and real animal facts with Ollie the Owl, in simple English.

Daqqa Kids Uploader is a small personal tool used only by the owner of the Daqqa Kids accounts. It uploads our own finished videos from our computer to our own TikTok account as drafts. We then review and publish each video ourselves inside the TikTok app. The tool is not offered to other users.
