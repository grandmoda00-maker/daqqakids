Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Daqqa Kids Uploader ("the tool") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Daqqa Kids accounts to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts.

Data we use

  • The access token that TikTok gives after the owner authorizes the tool, and the owner's TikTok open ID and display name.
  • The owner's own video files and captions.

How we use it

This data is used only to upload drafts to the owner's TikTok account and to check the status of those uploads. It is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Other people's data

The tool does not collect or process data of any other TikTok user or of viewers, including children.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app settings and delete the local token file. The tool keeps no other copies.
